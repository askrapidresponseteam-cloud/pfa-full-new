Is animal welfare a CSR activity?

Yes. Item (iv) of Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 names it, with environmental sustainability and the protection of flora and fauna.

What happens after we send this?
  1. A named person at PFA writes back.
  2. You agree a plan, with its cost and place.
  3. Your CSR committee approves it.
  4. PFA reports the work against the plan.
Can our people volunteer?

Yes, as part of a partnership. Say so in your note.

Can we fund something else?

Yes. Pick "Suggest one for us", or say what you have in mind.

What will you share for due diligence?

Ask in your note, and PFA will tell you what it can provide.
