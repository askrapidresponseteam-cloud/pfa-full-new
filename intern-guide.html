Internship

Getting started.

Two pages for every intern: how to get in, what to do each week, and how to send your work.

Download the PDF Open the portal

Getting in

You need: a phone or a computer with internet, and the email, mobile number and date of birth you applied with.

  1. Apply. On the internship page, give your email, mobile number and date of birth. These three become your sign-in.
  2. Wait for PFA's email. A person at PFA reads every application. When you are approved, you get an email called Your PFA internship has started. You cannot sign in before it.
  3. Sign in. Go to peopleforanimalsindia.org/intern. Type your email, your mobile number and your date of birth, then press Sign in.
  4. Every time after that, the same three. There is nothing else to write down or remember.

If it does not work

These details do not match an approved intern.
Check each one is exactly what you applied with: the same email, the 10-digit mobile number, the right date of birth.
Applied, but no email yet?
Your application is still with PFA. You can sign in once the internship has started email arrives.
Too many tries that did not match.
After five wrong tries in a row, sign-in rests for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again slowly.
Changed your email or number?
Write to PFA. PFA checks it is you and changes it.

Keep it safe

Anyone who knows your email, mobile number and date of birth together can sign in as you, so never share all three. PFA will never ask for them in a message. Press Sign out when you use someone else's phone or computer.

Your eight weeks

One task a week. Each is due on Sunday at 11:59 pm, India time. All eight make your certificate.

WeekWhat to doWhat to send
1Learn four animal laws and ten common cases. Then take the 30-question quiz in the portal: one hour.Nothing. Pass with 60% or more. Three tries, a day apart; the best counts.
2List every vet, government vet centre, shelter and ABC centre in your district, and at least 10 animal activists. Call each one to check.Your list with names, addresses and phone numbers, and dated photos of the government vet centres.
3Share your RRT link from your dashboard. Get 50 people in your district to join the RRT app.Nothing. Each person who finishes joining is counted for you.
4Make two short films for The Wall, about one minute each.The links to both films.
5 and 6Help at a shelter near you for 20 hours. First tell PFA which shelter, in the portal.Your hours, on a sheet the shelter signs.
6Send an RTI to your municipality about its dogs.The acknowledgement number.
7Nominate one film for CineKind.The nomination number.
8Write your own story with animals.400 to 600 words, with photographs.

How to send your work

  1. Sign in. Press the week, then press the task to open it.
  2. Paste a link (Google Drive, YouTube), one on each line, or attach photographs or a PDF, up to 3 MB each.
  3. Write a line or two for PFA, if you like. Press Send proof.

What happens next: the task shows Sent, with PFA. PFA then marks it Accepted with a score out of 10, or Revise with a note. For Revise, read the note, fix it, and press Send the revision.

Need help? Write to PFA at peopleforanimalsindia.org/ask. Always give the email you applied with.
