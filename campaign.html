What makes a campaign work?
  • One clear cause: nine dogs on one street.
  • A deadline: a birthday, a month, a race day.
  • One honest photograph.
  • A thank-you, telling people what their money did.
Can I collect money myself?

Not in PFA's name until PFA replies to your plan. Donations through PFA's donate page get an 80G receipt.

Can I collect supplies instead?

Yes. Choose Supplies as the goal; PFA tells you where they are needed.

Will PFA give me a poster or a letter?

Ask in your plan, and the reply will say what PFA can provide.
