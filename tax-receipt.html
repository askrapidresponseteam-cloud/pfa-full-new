Your PAN or Aadhaar, for the 80G receipt

Give one, with a photo of the card. It goes on your receipt, and PFA files it for your certificate.

Why we ask, and who sees it

PFA reports every 80G contribution to the Income Tax Department in its yearly statement of donations, with each donor's PAN or Aadhaar number. Your tax certificate is issued from that statement, and you need it to claim the deduction in your return. If the number is wrong by one character, the certificate does not reach you.

The photo lets PFA's accounts team check the number before it is filed; on an Aadhaar card, the QR code UIDAI prints on it is read to check the number as soon as you attach it. Only PFA's accounts team sees the photo. It is never sent to the payment gateway or anyone else, and an Aadhaar number appears on your receipt by its last four digits only.

A PAN is five letters, four digits and a letter, like ABCDE1234F.
An Aadhaar number is 12 digits.
