Colony Caregiver Card

      Check a card

      Type the number printed on the card, or scan the QR code on its back.

      What this page shows

      The holder's name, the card number, when it was issued and until when it is valid, and where the printed card is if one is on its way. Nothing else about the holder is shown here: not the photograph, the address or the mobile number. Those are on the printed card, which the holder carries, and the digital card here is its front. Lost yours? Ask us and quote the number.
