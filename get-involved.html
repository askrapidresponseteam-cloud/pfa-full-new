Colony caregiver

The card.

Feeding community dogs is lawful. The ABC Rules 2023 recognise caregivers, and the courts have held that a resident association cannot stop it.

The card is a document you can show when that is disputed. It is not a permit, and it does not give PFA any authority over your colony: it records that PFA knows who you are and what you do.

The card is issued free. The only charge is ₹50, for postage, to send the printed card to you.
