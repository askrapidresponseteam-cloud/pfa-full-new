Submit your guide

Three short steps.

One problem you have seen an animal through, told so that someone facing it now knows what to do. PFA reads every guide before it goes up, and emails you when it is published or if anything needs changing first.

What a guide can be about
  • An illness or injury: the signs, what the vet found, the treatment and the care at home.
  • First aid until a vet can be reached, and when it is an emergency.
  • Feeding, recovery after surgery or sterilisation, living with a disability, or caring for an old animal.
  • Street and community animals: what worked for the ones you look after.

Not for selling anything, a clinic’s advertisement, or a question about your own animal: for that, see a vet.
