# CSR outreach kit

Not part of the website. For whoever writes to companies about the projects on the CSR page.

## What is in here

- **csr-prospects.csv**: 40 companies with a record of funding animal welfare or close to it (dairy, agriculture, pharma, pet care, and mid-size companies), with the year, the amount where it is public, what they funded, which of PFA's projects fits them, and the source. Built from company CSR action plans, annual reports and news. The strongest leads: Dodla Dairy moved Rs 96.99 lakh earmarked for animal welfare into its unspent CSR account in 2025-26 because no project asked for it; Indian Immunologicals pledged 5 lakh rabies vaccine doses under CSR in August 2025.
- The eight one-page project PDFs are on the site: media/csr/ (linked from the CSR page under "Ready to fund").

## The official list: the MCA CSR portal

csr.gov.in > CSR data > Development sector: **Animal Welfare** > company-wise. It lists every company that booked CSR money to animal welfare, by year and amount. It could not be reached from where this kit was made, so pull it once and add the companies to the sheet. Filter for CSR budgets of Rs 2 crore to Rs 50 crore (mid-size) and for dairy, agriculture, pharma and pet companies.

## When to write

The CSR year ends on **31 March**. Money not spent by then must be moved: to the company's unspent CSR account by **30 April** if it is tied to an ongoing (multi-year) project, otherwise to a government fund by **30 September** (Companies Act, Section 135(5) and (6); missing it costs twice the amount or Rs 1 crore, whichever is less). So unspent budgets are committed from January to March, and a CSR committee needs a project in front of it before then.

1. **December**: pull the MCA list, pick 30 companies, send each the one or two PDFs that fit it with a short note.
2. **January**: follow up, offer a call, ask when their CSR committee meets.
3. **February to March**: sign. Every project runs 12 months, so it counts as an ongoing project: the company books it this year and moves the unspent part to its unspent CSR account by 30 April.
4. Report every quarter and at the close, with the numbers each PDF promises.

## Before sending

- Confirm the construction, kennel, cattle shelter, ultrasound, lab and van fit-out lines with local quotes; they are estimates. Wages use Delhi's April 2026 minimum rates, diesel Rs 90 a litre.
- Have ready: CSR-1 registration with MCA, 12A and 80G, and audited accounts. Companies cannot fund an agency without CSR-1.
- The sterilisation project needs a PFA unit recognised by the Animal Welfare Board for this work and the local body's agreement. The X-ray needs AERB registration (in the price). Village vet assistants give first aid and support under a vet's supervision.
