What counts, and why
  • Only a finished registration on the RRT app counts toward your target. Opening your link, visiting the store or opening the app are shown so you can see where people stop; they are never counted as joins.
  • On Android, the Play Store passes your code to the app when it is installed from your link, and a phone that already has the app opens it straight from the link.
  • On an iPhone, the App Store does not pass a code on. Ask them to type your code when they register.
  • A registration is credited once, to one code, and never moved. Your own number is never counted for you. You never see who registered.
