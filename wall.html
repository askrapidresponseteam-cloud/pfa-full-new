How it works
01

Paste the link

Copy the address of your reel, post or video. The post has to be public, or the wall cannot reach it.

02

An editor watches it

Someone at PFA watches every submission. Nothing that identifies a rescue address, nothing about a live case, and no footage of an animal being hurt. If you have that, it is evidence: report it instead, where it stays private and reaches a named person.

03

It goes up on the wall

Approved pieces publish under your name with a link back to the original post. Ask us to take it down at any time and it comes down.
