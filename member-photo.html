Hello.

PFA needs one photograph of you for your membership card.

What to change

    How to take a good photograph
    • Stand in front of a plain, light wall, in daylight.
    • Face the camera straight on, shoulders up.
    • No sunglasses, cap or mask. Glasses you always wear are fine.

    Drag to place your face in the frame. Use the slider to zoom.

    Used only to print your card, and kept with your membership record.

    Thank you.

    Your photograph is with PFA for approval. You will get an email when your card is approved, and the printed card is sent to you by courier.

    Member number

    Follow your card

    Need help? Contact PFA and quote your member number.
