Open what you need.

What happens inside?
  • Wards, open 24 hours.
  • Out-patients, 24 hours, with an animal ambulance at night.
  • Intensive care, for large animals and small.
  • Operation theatre, the hub of animal birth control.
  • Laboratory, with X-ray and blood tests.
  • Sterilisation and vaccination for the city's dogs.
  • Gaushala, for the cattle in its care.
  • Animal cemetery, one of very few in Delhi.
